Changes made: each SVG was rasterized to a 64x64 32-bit TGA, its black background square was removed (alpha taken from luminance), and the art was recolored white so the client can tint it. Names that collide between authors carry the author's folder name as a suffix (<name>-<author>), and names with a British spelling were respelled in US English (see US_RULES in tools/artwork/icon_cleaner.py).

Every mark's author is listed in AUTHORS.txt beside this file.
